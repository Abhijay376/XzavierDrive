Converting Your Indian Driving Licence to a Victorian Licence

India is classed as an Experienced Driver Recognition country, meaning your pathway depends on your age and years of driving experience. Here's exactly what applies to you.

Step-by-step conversion process

  • Get your documents ready Valid Indian licence, passport, proof of Victorian address (lease or utility bill).
  • Arrange a translation if needed If your licence isn't in English, get a NAATI accredited translation or a valid International Driving Permit.
  • Book a VicRoads appointment Appointments fill quickly — book a few weeks ahead.
  • Complete any required tests If you're under 25, this includes a knowledge test and practical driving test.
  • Practice on local roads first Indian traffic norms differ significantly from Victorian road rules — roundabouts, lane discipline, and give-way rules are common problem areas.
  • Surrender your Indian licence You cannot hold both simultaneously once converted.

Common challenges for Indian licence holders

Based on the students Anjali has worked with, the most common adjustment areas are: roundabout lane discipline, mandatory full stops at stop signs, and stricter following distances than typically practiced in India.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert my Indian driving licence directly in Victoria?

It depends on your age. If you are 25 or older and have held your licence for 3+ years, direct exchange is often possible. Under 25s generally need to pass a knowledge and practical test.

Do I need an English translation of my Indian licence?

Yes, if your licence is not in English you will need a NAATI accredited translation or an International Driving Permit that meets VicRoads requirements.
