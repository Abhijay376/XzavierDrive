Find out in 10 seconds whether you can convert your licence directly, need a knowledge test, or must complete the full VicRoads driving test.

How Victoria classifies overseas licences

Recognised countries

Similar driving standards to Australia. Most full licence holders can exchange directly.

  • UK, Ireland, Canada
  • Germany, France, Japan
  • No test usually required

Experienced Driver Recognition

Partial recognition. Requirements depend on your age and years of driving experience.

  • India, USA, Philippines
  • Under 25s usually need tests
  • 25+ may exchange directly

Other countries

Full Victorian licensing process applies, including learner and test stages.

  • Knowledge test
  • Hazard perception test
  • Practical driving test
